The AI Lab · OpenDebt showcase

Governed delivery, made visible

Four interactive prototypes for how the Lab thinks about AI in software delivery — loops, human gates, traceability and architecture you can actually check. The Foundry sort hub started this; here are more.

AI already shortens time-to-first-code. The harder problem is delivering complex, regulated systems without losing control. These views make one idea tangible each: that acceleration comes from less ambiguity, less rework and less drift — with human judgment concentrated where it counts. Open any one to explore it live.

Language we hold to

Say this

  • Governed delivery
  • Artifact-level traceability
  • Cross-cutting NFRs
  • Architecture as a checked artifact
  • Documentation drift is actively controlled
  • Human judgment concentrated at the critical gates

Not this

  • Full traceability of every decision
  • Documentation is always perfect
  • Fully autonomous delivery
  • The agents just build the system for you
  • Developers are no longer needed

Built on the EY Motif palette. Each prototype is a self-contained HTML file — open it, embed it, or fork it into the next idea. The point isn't magical perfection; it's making drift, ambiguity and rework hard to hide.
